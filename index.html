Scale Contrast Theory: Invariant Negotiation

Scale Contrast Theory offers a grammar of cited observation. Every quantity can be offered as a distance read against a distance. When the two seats are spatial and temporal the contrast takes the form ρ = Δx/(cΔt). The contribution is the invariant equation reading = κ · I · Cᵖ and the audit format that records the standing of each reading.

The disparity is the relation itself, not a relation among isolated terms. Distance is expressed due to scale/ratio disparity. The negotiation is a disparity of expression, offering expression. Expression is relative to distance. Distance contrasted can only offer distance.

ρ = Δx / (c Δt)

A ratio is not a measure applied to two distances. It is the disparity itself when one contrast is faced against the other. Δx and cΔt are not inputs. They are the two seats of the same facing. ρ is that facing held as one. There is no operation that produces the ratio. The ratio is the expression of the disparity.

Δx is a spatial contrast. cΔt is a temporal contrast — the distance light spans in the facing. c is the constant mode of the disparity when the disparity is read as constant. ρ is the reading: one contrast held relative to another.

No external producer: the expression is closed; any label enters only after the reading.

The seat is co-given with the reading. Orientation offers the scale and the sign. The rate is held across the seats. Being and becoming are the same contrast read at the now. The now is not a moment on a line — it is the slice an orientation reads as Δt = 0.

The paper does not replace the legacy formalism or predict new results; it reads the same results as expressions of one relation.

The form

The canvas is the span, cΔt: the distance light spans in the facing. The mark is the separation, Δx. ρ persists under rescaling of units; a change of relative motion changes ρ except at ρ = 1. Where ρ ≤ 1 the separation is within reach. Where ρ > 1 it is beyond reach.

ρ = 1 is the constant mode of the disparity. Mark and canvas are the same, so the relative change is zero. A constant is the unread limit of that mode — the seat at which the disparity offers no further distinction. Beyond or within reach, order is offered by orientation alone. Only the light edge and the side of the light-cone are preserved across orientations.

The legacy already uses ratio condensation. The gauge pressure at depth is the weight of the column over the area, so the ratio of the gauge pressure at 10 meters to that at 5 meters is 2. The legacy accepts this form. The grammar extends it: the same ratio condensation is the form of every measurement.

The invariant equation

reading = κ · I · Cᵖ

The reading is the coefficient κ times the held invariant I times the canvas C raised to the exponent p. The local scale is the invariant’s seat; the scale is the canvas. The exponent p is the relative of the local scale to the scale.

The exponent is offered, not derived. The equation classifies seven cited cases (solar orbits, a whirlpool outside and inside its core, the flat galactic curve, local freefall, inverse-square gravity and the Casimir plates) by the exponent each seat offers, and checks that every member of a seat returns the same invariant. An eighth case, a string wound on a tube, marks the boundary of the equation.

The audit

Kind classifies each row:

  • R: algebra on the legacy form or a closed relative (a rewrite; cannot fail).
  • I: needs a seated input beyond the definitions.
  • A: an argument. A row is an argument if its form yields a functional dependence, a structurally different form yields a different dependence, and the two can be told apart by measurement.

Standing records where each row stands:

  • Held: the measurement is cited, and the row returns it.
  • Untested: no measurement is specified, or the comparison is not made here.
  • Accurate alignment: the row restates the legacy’s form (the algebra, the standard result, the closed relation) in the grammar’s terms. It is accurate as an alignment, not a claim that cannot fail.
  • Conditionally aligned: the offered value falls within the observation’s stated systematic error but not its random error.
  • Not a test: an interpretive position.
  • Mark met: a measurement the Mark describes has been reported, so the row fails as stated. No row currently has this standing.

The Marks. Each states a measurement that would count against the grammar as offered here.

  • Mark 1 (commitment): a quantity that cannot be offered as a distance read against a distance through seated constants.
  • Mark 2 (solar seat): a member of the solar seat returning a seated length that differs beyond its own perturbation budget.
  • Mark 3 (whirlpool): a bench vortex outside its core in which r v is not held.
  • Mark 4 (Mercury): a relativistic precession that scales as 1/q² rather than 1/q across orbits of different shape.
  • Mark 5 (a₀): a measured acceleration scale that stays the same across redshift, so that a₀ does not follow H(z).

The spectrum’s ends

The spectrum has a ceiling and a floor. The ceiling is the light edge, ρ = 1, shared by every seat. The floor is 1/2—the minimal contrast, the binary of contrast. The Planck length is the length formed from the seated constants—a concept, not a claimed limit. The floor is the 1/2, seated; the Planck length is the length, cited. Whether spacetime has a smallest increment is an open question.

One whole, many seats

The whole turn is written 360. Any N is a seat. 360 offers a simulation of a complete expression: enough divisors that the discrete steps remain whole numbers. It is not a privileged number. ρ per step is invariant; only the label changes.

The continuous turn is the circle. Because π is irrational, the circle and its discrete readings share no common unit. Every inscribed n-gon therefore leaves a unit-free gap

1 − n sin(π/n)/π.

The gap is the shortfall of the perimeter as a fraction of the whole. At n=6 it is 1−3/π≈0.045. As n grows the gap falls; the circle is the limit no finite seat reaches. It is the unread seat, and every polygon is a reading of it. 360 lets the simulation close under ordinary counting; the circle remains the continuous offer the discrete seats only approach.

Time

Time is a metric. It is always read at the now.

Time is not a dimension beside distance. It is the canvas a separation is read against: temporal scale is distance scaled. The now is the slice an orientation reads as Δt = 0. It is a relative-to, not a moment on a line. The contrast of two times is a time, and no instant stands alone.

Declined priors

  • No arrow.
  • No causal production.
  • Labels may be attached after the reading; none is required to form it.

ρ is a magnitude, the same for Δt and −Δt. It gives reach, not order. Orientation offers the sign; the grammar does not derive the arrow. A later mark is not read as produced by an earlier one. Causality, if the word is wanted, is the now's: a rate held across seats and continued. The reading is offered; the route may be constituted; the law is declined.

Examples

Freefall. The held invariant is the acceleration and the reading is the speed. The reading is the speed, v ∝ t; the acceleration a is the held invariant. With time as canvas, v = a t, so p = +1 and κ = 1. With height as canvas the same fall holds v²/h constant, v = √(2a h), so p = +1/2 and κ = √2. The two canvases are related by squaring.

Canvas: the disparity. Rate: the room's rate, read from freefall. Mark: the height fallen, h, read against the whole height, H, as the elapsed time, t, is read against the whole time, T.

h / H = (t / T)²

Equal time seats are faced. Distance marks grow as the square. The magnitude is seated at the whole. The label follows after. The square is the same expression, not a second rate.

The continuation holds while the rate is one rate, and stops where a second rate, drag, enters.

The planets hold r v². Taking r as the orbit’s semi-major axis and v = 2πr/T, every planet returns the same seated length L = 2 r v² / c² ≈ 2.953 km (r v²/c² ≈ 1.48 km) within the precision of mean orbital elements (Williams n.d.); the giant planets agree less closely, because their own masses and mutual pulls enter the elements. The agreement is Kepler’s observed regularity, not a constant imposed from outside. The legacy names the reading GM; the grammar reads the length, and the label is after.

The inner four planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars) agree to four significant figures: L = 2.953 km and rv² = 1.327 × 10²⁰ m³s⁻² in every case. Orbital regard falls by roughly a factor of two between Mercury and Mars, and the product holds steady.

Across the galactic seats the speed is held, so ρ = v/c is held. The negotiation held will see the seated length rise in proportion to r. This is a seeing, not an empirical match: it restates the flat curve rather than accounting for it, and the audit grades it R. The legacy labels the rise enclosed mass growing with radius, or dark matter; the grammar reads the length rising and does not seat a substance.

The a₀ candidate. The candidate offers the acceleration scale a₀ = c H₀ / 2π. With H₀ = 67.4 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹ (Planck Collaboration 2020) the offer is 1.04 × 10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻²; with H₀ = 73.0 (Riess et al. 2022) it is 1.13 × 10⁻¹⁰. The observed scale is 1.20 ± 0.02 (random) ± 0.24 (systematic) × 10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻² (McGaugh, Lelli & Schombert 2016). The offer falls within the systematic error but not the random error: conditionally aligned, not a hit and not a miss. The testable part is the consequence that a₀ follows H(z), and so should be larger at earlier times; the evolution of the relation with redshift is now being measured (Ciocan et al. 2026).

The pie offer

Any negotiation may be expressed as a 360 pie. The pie is the whole. The sectors are the seats. The reading is the sector against the whole.

  • Canvas: the whole. 360.
  • Sectors: the seats.
  • Reading: the sector against the whole.

The pie is the containment that shows how the same disparity returns. Linear seats are the mark; the square is that mark faced against itself. One expression appears once as the equal divisions and once as their square. The pie closes on that return. Nothing is added.

The pie is not a sequence. It is the whole, divided. It can be read forwards or backwards; the direction is the orientation. The sectors do not change. The reading is the same.

The pie is recursive. The degree does not change. What changes is the relative expression.

A change of rate does not open a new canvas; the canvas remains, and only the marks, the later sectors, are translated.

A running 360 pie incorporates its variables as the shifting degrees (for example R/v); the degrees must continue to balance to the whole.

The disparity and its constant

The family

SCT is one grammar in a relational family. The family includes Nāgārjuna’s emptiness, Whitehead’s process, structural realism, and more recent relational grammars (Wade’s Projected Operational Grammar, August 2026). The primitives differ (SCT’s is the relative-to; Wade’s is the Address; Nāgārjuna’s is emptiness); the stance is the same (the relation is primitive; no producer). SCT’s contribution is the relative-to, the audit format, and the checkable math.
