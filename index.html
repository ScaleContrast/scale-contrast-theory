Scale Contrast Theory

Everything is read as contrast, relative to.

The form

ρ = Δx / (c Δt)

Δx is a spatial contrast. Δt is a temporal contrast. c is the reference rate. ρ is the reading: one contrast held relative to another.

Time

Time is a metric. It is always read at the now.

Declined priors

  • No arrow.
  • No causal production.
  • Mass and force are labels, applied after the reading.

Examples

Freefall is a held rate.

Orbit is a closed relative-to.

Papers

Scale Contrast Theory: Now as Relative-To by William P. Robinson. The primary statement of SCT's grammar: contrast read relative-to, at the now.

Questions

What moves?
Nothing is asked to move. A contrast is read, Δx relative to c Δt.
Where is the past?
Not held. Every reading is taken at the now.
What does gravity do?
Nothing. Freefall is a rate held; force is a label put on it after.
