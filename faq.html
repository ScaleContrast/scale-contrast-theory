Questions

Answers in the grammar of Now as Relative-To.

What is the relative-to?

It is the now. Think of it as the slice an orientation reads as Δt = 0. It is not a moment sitting on a line. Every contrast is simply read against it.

What is ρ = Δx / (c Δt)?

It is the facing of one separation. The mark is the separation Δx. The canvas is the span cΔt — the distance light spans in that facing. Where ρ ≤ 1 the separation is within reach. Where ρ > 1 it is beyond reach. The ratio itself does not change when the seat changes.

Why is there no arrow?

ρ is only a magnitude. It reads the same for Δt and −Δt. It tells you reach, not which way things run. Orientation is what offers the sign. The grammar does not try to derive an arrow.

Why is there no causal production?

A later mark is not read as something an earlier mark produced. If the word “causality” is wanted, it belongs to the now: a rate held across seats and continued. The grammar declines a law that sits outside the facing.

How is freefall read?

As a held rate. The canvas is the disparity. The rate is the room’s rate, read from freefall. The mark is the height fallen, h, set against the whole height H, while the elapsed time t is set against the whole time T.

h / H = (t / T)²

No constant is seated first. g = 2H/T² is just a label placed after the reading. The continuation ends where a second rate, such as drag, enters.

How is an orbit read?

As a closed relative-to. One full turn at 1 AU is one year. Take the year as the unit of time and the AU as the unit of distance, and the tally the room returns is K = v²r = 4π². It is not fetched from anywhere. No mass, no G. It is closed because the engagement meets itself after the full facing: the year tally returns K = 4π². It is open because the rate is held, not produced from a prior. The constant is simply the relative-to that persists across the seats.

Why do the outer planets drift?

One invariant, relative expression. In AU/year units the closed turn returns K = v²r = 4π² exactly for uniform offerings (inner planets). For Saturn the rings constitute a distinct offering — two scales held as one station. The same closed turn is therefore read relative to that offering:

K = 4π² (1 + δ)

where δ is the relative factor supplied by the two-scale station. 4π² itself does not change; the expression of the return is relative to the offering. The drift is that relative factor, not a different constant and not an unabsorbed remainder.

The relative factor is read from the offering’s own structure. The main rings span approximately 1.16 Rp. That dimensionless ratio is the mark of the second scale against the body. Faced as a correction of the same order as the observed fractional departure, it accounts for the few-percent drift without introducing a new constant or a second closed turn. The tally remains the single invariant read at each seat — exact for uniform offerings, relative for the distinct one.

(The precise numerical mapping from the span ratio onto the exact percentage is a further relative reading internal to the station; it is not required for the seating, and no independent geometric factor is claimed to fix it in advance.)

How is a capacitor read?

The room is a capacitor through a resistor. The canvas is the room’s own time L, dual-named RC after the reading. The mark is the charge ratio ρσ = q/q₀. The rows show ρσ = e^(−ρt) and the disparity property ρσ(t₁ + t₂) = ρσ(t₁) · ρσ(t₂). The thinning over a disparity does not depend on where that disparity began. Orientation seats the sign; the equation itself does not. A second rate ends the continuation.

Where is the past?

“This and not that” does not disregard this or that. The contrast, or the negotiation, is what is now. This time not that time is now. Earlier and later remain the two ends of the disparity; what is read at the seat is their contrast. The now does not replace the past; it is the reading in which they are already negotiated.

What do mass and force do?

Nothing in the reading. They are labels placed after — names the reading does not need. No mass is isolated, no force is yielded, no G is seated as a prior.

What is time?

Distance scaled. Time is not a separate dimension sitting beside distance. It is the canvas a separation is read against. The contrast of two times is itself a time. No instant stands alone.

What is a seat?

A naming of the canvas and the mark before the ratio is read. 360, 2π, 400 and 1 are simply different seats for one whole. The seat changes the label and the number of steps, not the whole and not the relation.

Why is the whole written 360?

Any N can be a seat. 360 has enough divisors that the discrete steps stay whole numbers. ρ per step is invariant; only the label changes. See One whole, many seats.

What is the pie offer?

Any negotiation can be expressed as a 360 pie. The pie is the whole. The sectors are the seats. The reading is the sector against the whole. See The pie offer.

What is a canvas?

What a mark is read against. For the now it is the span cΔt. No disparity can be seated without a named canvas.

What is a mark?

What is read: a fraction of a whole, ρ = mark/whole. Every claim also ends with a mark — the reading that would separate it.

What is c?

c is the constant mode of the disparity when the disparity is read as constant. The disparity is the relative-to itself. See The disparity and its constant.

What is reach?

ρ ≤ 1. Within reach, order holds across orientations. Beyond reach, order is offered by the orientation alone.

Is the theory claiming legacy physics is wrong?

No. The rows are the same under both paths. The paper claims the path is open, not that the other is false. The reading is offered. See Consistency of the tally.
